Capabilities

What the assistant does for you

Eight connected capabilities that turn a busy inbox into an organised, actionable workload.

Monitors incoming emails

Watches your Gmail inbox (or selected labels) and picks up new messages as they arrive, on a schedule you choose.

Summarises messages

Turns long emails and threads into short, clear summaries so you understand each message in seconds.

Detects priority

Rates each email as high, medium or low priority — with a short reason — based on deadlines, senders and content.

Extracts actionable tasks

Pulls out requests and deadlines and turns them into tasks with due dates in your tracker.

Prepares email responses

Drafts replies in a professional tone for you to review, edit and approve before sending.

Tracks follow-ups

Records follow-up dates and reminds you when a conversation needs attention again.

Supports calendar management

Spots meeting requests and suggests calendar entries or reschedules for your confirmation.

Generates daily briefings

Prepares a short daily overview: priorities, tasks due, calendar changes and drafts awaiting approval.

Workflow diagram

How the system fits together

Gmail → Make.com → AI Processing → Google Sheets / Google Calendar → Human Approval

Step 1

Gmail

Where your emails arrive. The system reads new messages from your inbox.

Step 2

Make.com

The automation engine. It detects new emails and passes them through each step in order.

Step 3

AI Processing

An AI language model reads the email and returns a summary, priority, tasks and a draft reply.

Step 4

Sheets / Calendar

Results are logged to a Google Sheets dashboard; meetings and reminders go to Google Calendar.

Step 5

Human Approval

You review drafts and suggestions, then approve, edit or reject. Nothing is sent without you.

In simple language

The role of each technology

No jargon — here's what each part of the system does.

Gmail — your inbox

Nothing changes about how people email you. The assistant simply has permission to read new messages and, once you approve, send replies from your account.

Make.com — the connector

Make.com is a visual automation platform. Think of it as a set of instructions: when a new email arrives, do these steps in this order. It connects Gmail, the AI and Google's apps without custom servers.

AI processing — the reader

An AI language model handles the reading and writing work: summarising, judging urgency, spotting tasks and drafting a reply. It follows instructions tailored to your business and tone.

Google Sheets — the dashboard

Every processed email becomes a row: sender, summary, priority, tasks, follow-up date and status. It's simple, shareable and easy to filter or report on.

Google Calendar — the schedule

Meeting requests, reminders and follow-ups can be added as calendar suggestions, so your schedule reflects what's in your inbox.

Human approval — you

The final decision is always yours. You approve, edit or reject drafts and suggestions. This keeps quality high and protects your relationships.

Questions

About the AI Executive Assistant

Does it work with Google Workspace business accounts?

Yes. It works with both personal Gmail and Google Workspace accounts. Workspace admin settings may need to allow the connection.

Can it reply in my tone of voice?

Yes. The AI is given instructions and examples that reflect how you write, and you can edit any draft before approving it.

What happens if the AI gets something wrong?

Because drafts and suggestions require approval, mistakes are caught before they reach anyone. You can reject or edit, and recurring issues are used to refine the instructions.

How often does it check my inbox?

That depends on your Make.com plan and needs — for example every 15 minutes or hourly. Frequency affects usage costs, so we choose a sensible schedule together.
