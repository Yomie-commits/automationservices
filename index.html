FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What kind of tasks can be automated?

Repetitive, rule-based or text-heavy work is usually a good fit: sorting and summarising email, logging enquiries, updating Google Sheets, creating tasks from requests, sending follow-up reminders and preparing draft replies. During the consultation we identify which of your tasks are worth automating.

Will the AI send emails without my approval?

No — by default, AI-drafted replies wait for human approval before anything is sent. You stay in control of what goes out in your name. Fully automatic sending can be considered only for low-risk messages, if you request it.

Which tools do you work with?

Core systems are built with Gmail, Google Sheets, Google Calendar and Make.com, with an AI model for summarising, classifying and drafting. Other apps can often be connected through Make.com — ask about your specific tools.

Do I need technical knowledge?

No. I handle the setup and testing, then train you or your team and provide simple documentation explaining how the system works and what to do if something needs attention.

Is my business data safe?

Automations run inside your own accounts (for example your Google Workspace and Make.com), with access limited to what each workflow needs. We agree what data is processed before building, and you can revoke access at any time.

How long does a setup take?

It depends on the scope. A focused email automation can be quicker than a multi-integration business workflow. You'll receive a timeline with your proposal after the assessment.

Are there ongoing costs?

Apart from my fee, some tools have their own subscriptions or usage-based costs (for example Make.com operations and AI model usage). These are explained upfront so there are no surprises. Optional monthly maintenance is also available.

Do you work with clients outside Kenya?

Yes. I'm based in Nairobi and work remotely, so I can support clients in Kenya and internationally. Consultations can be held by video call or WhatsApp.
