SERVICE 01

AI Executive Assistant Systems

An AI-supported assistant that reads, summarises and prioritises your email, extracts tasks, drafts replies and prepares a daily briefing.

Enquire about this

The business problem

Business owners and executives lose hours every day reading, sorting and responding to email. Important requests get buried, deadlines are missed and nobody has a clear picture of what needs attention first.

The solution

A connected assistant system that monitors your inbox, summarises each message, flags what is urgent, turns requests into tracked tasks and prepares draft responses — all reviewed and approved by you before anything is sent.

How the automation works

  1. New emails in Gmail trigger a Make.com scenario.
  2. An AI model summarises the message, assigns a priority and extracts action items.
  3. Results are logged to a Google Sheets dashboard and, where relevant, Google Calendar.
  4. Draft replies wait for your approval; a daily briefing summarises what matters.

Expected operational benefits

  • Less time spent reading and triaging email
  • Urgent messages surface first
  • Requests become trackable tasks
  • A clear daily overview of your workload

SERVICE 02

Email Management Automation

Gmail automation that sorts, labels, summarises and routes messages so your inbox stays organised without constant manual effort.

Enquire about this

The business problem

Shared and personal inboxes fill up with enquiries, invoices, notifications and internal messages. Sorting them manually is slow and inconsistent, and important emails are easy to miss.

The solution

Rules-plus-AI email management: messages are automatically categorised, labelled, summarised and routed to the right place or person, with priority alerts for anything time-sensitive.

How the automation works

  1. Gmail filters and Make.com watch for new messages.
  2. AI classifies each email (e.g. lead, invoice, support, internal) and writes a short summary.
  3. Emails are labelled, forwarded or logged according to your rules.
  4. High-priority messages trigger an alert (e.g. email or chat notification).

Expected operational benefits

  • A consistently organised inbox
  • Faster handling of time-sensitive email
  • Less manual sorting and forwarding
  • A searchable log of important correspondence

Client and Lead Management

Capture every enquiry automatically, log it in one place, reply quickly and follow up on time — so no potential client is forgotten.

Enquire about this

The business problem

Enquiries arrive through email, website forms and messaging. Without a system, leads are recorded inconsistently, replies are slow and follow-ups depend on memory.

The solution

An enquiry pipeline that captures new leads automatically, records them in a central tracker, prepares a personalised first response and schedules follow-up reminders until the lead is closed.

How the automation works

  1. Website forms and enquiry emails feed into Make.com.
  2. Lead details are extracted and added to a Google Sheets tracker.
  3. A tailored acknowledgement is drafted for your approval.
  4. Follow-up reminders are scheduled based on the lead's status.

Expected operational benefits

  • Every enquiry captured in one place
  • Faster first responses
  • Consistent follow-up
  • Clear visibility of your pipeline

Calendar and Task Automation

Turn emails and requests into scheduled tasks, track deadlines and get reminders — with calendar suggestions ready for your approval.

Enquire about this

The business problem

Meeting requests, deadlines and to-dos are scattered across email threads and notes. Rescheduling takes back-and-forth, and deadlines slip when tasks aren't tracked.

The solution

Automation that extracts dates and deadlines from messages, creates tasks with due dates, suggests calendar entries and sends reminders before things fall due.

How the automation works

  1. AI identifies meeting requests, dates and deadlines in incoming messages.
  2. Tasks are created in a tracker with owners and due dates.

  4. Reminders are sent ahead of deadlines and follow-up dates.

Expected operational benefits

  • Deadlines tracked automatically
  • Less scheduling back-and-forth
