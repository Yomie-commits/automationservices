Pricing FAQ

Common pricing questions

Why is the price a starting point?

Final pricing depends on workflow complexity, the number of integrations, expected usage and implementation requirements. You'll receive a fixed quote after the assessment, before any work starts.

Are third-party tool costs included?

No. Tools such as Make.com and AI model providers have their own plans and usage-based charges, paid directly by you from your own accounts. I'll recommend the most cost-effective option for your volume.

Is usage unlimited?

No. Every automation runs within the limits of the plans you choose (for example Make.com operations and AI usage). We size the system to your expected volume and explain what happens if it grows.

What does monthly maintenance include?

Monitoring, fixing issues if a connected app changes, small adjustments to AI instructions and workflows, and periodic check-ins. The exact scope is agreed in writing.

Do you work with international clients?

Yes. Prices are shown in Kenyan shillings; international clients can request a quote in USD or another currency.
