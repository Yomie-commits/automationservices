My story

Technology should give you time back

I'm Naomi Mwangi, an AI Automation Specialist based in Nairobi, Kenya. My background spans information and communication technology, web development, digital marketing, virtual assistance and AI data training and annotation — work that has shown me, from several angles, how much time businesses spend on repetitive administration.

Having worked on the administrative side of businesses, I know how quickly email, enquiries, follow-ups and spreadsheets can take over a working day. My focus is helping businesses use technology to simplify those operations — designing automation that is practical, reliable and easy to understand, with people kept in control of important decisions.

I hold a Diploma in Information Communication Technology and work remotely with clients and teams, both in Kenya and internationally. I work in English and Kiswahili, with conversational French.
