How your information is handled when you contact me through this website.

Who I am

This website is operated by Naomi Mwangi, AI Automation Services, based in Nairobi, Kenya. Contact: see contact page.

What information is collected

When you submit the enquiry or consultation form, the details you provide are collected: name, business name, email address, phone number (optional), business type, automation needs, budget range, preferred meeting details and any additional information you choose to share.

How it is used

Your information is used only to respond to your enquiry, arrange consultations and prepare proposals. It is not sold or shared for marketing purposes.

How it is processed and stored

Form submissions are delivered through the website's form service [Netlify Forms / Formspree / FormSubmit — keep the one you use] and by email. Information is kept only as long as needed to respond and for reasonable business records.

Your rights

In line with the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019, you may request access to, correction of or deletion of your personal data at any time by contacting me.

Client automation projects

Automations built for clients run in the client's own accounts. The data processed, access granted and retention are agreed with each client before any work begins.
